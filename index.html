What is PLINKO and how does the board work?

What is plinko? In plain terms, it is a chance-based game built around a triangular board of pegs. Each time the ball hits a peg, it can go left or right, and that repeated split creates the final result. The more rows you use, the more pockets there are and the more spread out the outcomes become.

That pattern is why PLINKO feels easy to follow even when the results vary. Most balls collect near the middle, while the outer pockets are harder to hit and usually pay more. In a plinko casino setting, that balance is the core of the game design.
